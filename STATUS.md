# Fact-Level Consistency Benchmark v1

**State:** frozen historical reference.

This benchmark contains 160 application-evaluation cases and 192 annotated facts. The included `snapshots/` directory is the original frozen archive and manifest; its manifest records hashes for the precise source, data, lore index, prompts and reports used at freeze time.

The latest complete result predates the addition of performance instrumentation. Do not interpret short GPU validation as a replacement for the original complete 160-case result.
